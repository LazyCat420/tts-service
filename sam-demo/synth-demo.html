From-scratch voice synthesiser

Formant synthesis written from the literature: a rule-based reciter, a Fujisaki-style prosody model, a Klatt parallel formant bank excited by a period-synchronous impulse train, then de-essing, LTAS tilt, CELP postfilter and BS.1770 loudness. numpy only, no model weights.
